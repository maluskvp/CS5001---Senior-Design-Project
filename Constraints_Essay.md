---

## 💰 Economic

As college students, we are limited financially and will depend directly on what is available through the professors we plan to work with. We currently don't have the funds, personal or company, to purchase items that would benefit our testing or development processes. We will be limited to what is available in UC labs and what we have on hand: mobile phones, personal laptops, webcams, free development tools, and so on.

## 🤝 Social

Both of our project ideas would have a direct social impact.

- **Professor Seward's project, See Words,** would directly impact children's education when it comes to reading. We would have the opportunity to fine-tune or create a new way for students to learn to read and write effectively, with visual depictions of the letters and audible phonetics.
- **Professor Tang's project** would impact UC's research by allowing professors and students to observe different 3D models in an understandable setting, where they can move the model and read information about it in a simple, automated way.

## 🎓 Professional

Our individual professional development would grow on either project.

- **With Professor Seward,** we would work directly on improving an already patented product that has a direct positive impact on children learning to read.
- **With Professor Tang,** we would be improving UC's research on 3D modeling and data visualization in a way that can be automated, and we would work closely with someone who has experience and expertise in AI and AR/XR.

Either project would require 3D modeling, computer vision, AR/XR, and possibly some AI expertise. As a group made up entirely of Computer Science majors, we understand the CS concepts both projects require, but we would need help connecting CS with model rendering and automation.

## ⚖️ Legal

See Words is owned and patented by Professor Seward, so as a group we would be required to sign legal documents before being allowed to work on her product.
